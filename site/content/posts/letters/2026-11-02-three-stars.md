title: Three Stars
---

*(What follows is my review of the Hyatt Place in Herndon, VA, exactly [as published on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qzGgiM6epDfCVpeB7).)*

My hotel room is a hotel room as remembered by the Backrooms. 

The TV is on the wall off to the side to the left of the beds.

In front of the beds is the sink. The sink area is carpeted.

The refrigerator door can’t be closed because the shelf on the door doesn’t fit in the refrigerator. It’s like someone broke the door off and then replaced it with the door from another fridge.

There is a big empty space where something should be but is not.

Signs in the elevator assure me the buttons do work, even if they don't appear to. Please, be gentle. 

The toilet makes much noise and does so little.

In the morning, a sign informed me there are no scrambled eggs at breakfast because the “breakfast machine” is broken. There are potatoes and sausage. I wonder what machine made those.

I located the source of the white noise I could not place the night before. The phone had been on speaker since my arrival. Listening.

The signs in the elevator describe the breakfast as "thoughtfully sourced." They do not elaborate on the thoughts.

As I depart, the fire alarm panel near the entrance attempts to draw attention to some “trouble.” It is ignored.
